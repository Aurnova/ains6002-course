# AINS6002: Machine Learning & Predictive Modeling

**Aurnova MSAI track:** Core  
**Credits:** 3  
**Format:** 8-week online graduate course

Covers supervised, unsupervised, and operational predictive modeling with reproducible evaluation.

This public cohort [repository](glossary.md#repository) includes student-facing course materials: book prose, assignment [notebooks](glossary.md#notebook), slide notebooks, narration, rubrics, and executable labs.

## Course Outcomes

By the end of the course, students will be able to:

- explain the major concepts and tradeoffs in [Machine Learning](glossary.md#machine-learning) & Predictive Modeling;
- build or evaluate applied AI artifacts aligned with the course domain;
- document assumptions, evidence, limitations, and operational [risks](glossary.md#risk);
- connect technical work to [governance](glossary.md#governance), stakeholder needs, and [deployment](glossary.md#deployment) readiness.

## Module Map

1. **Prediction tasks and data framing** — What decision is the [model](glossary.md#model) actually supporting?
2. **Data preparation and [feature](glossary.md#feature) pipelines** — How do preprocessing choices shape model behavior?
3. **Linear and tree-based [baselines](glossary.md#baseline)** — Why do strong baselines matter before complex models?
4. **Model selection and [validation](glossary.md#validation)** — How do we choose models without [overfitting](glossary.md#overfitting) our evidence?
5. **Unsupervised learning and structure discovery** — How can models reveal patterns without labels?
6. **Time, drift, and [monitoring](glossary.md#monitoring)** — What changes after a model leaves the notebook?
7. **Interpretability and stakeholder explanation** — What explanations are appropriate for different audiences?
8. **Predictive modeling portfolio** — What evidence supports a production recommendation?

### What Is Interactive?

The readings and rendered slides are navigable web pages. The lab and assignment notebooks are the interactive elements: launch a lab in Colab or [Codespaces](glossary.md#codespaces), execute [cells](glossary.md#cell), change a named [input](glossary.md#input), and interpret the resulting evidence. “RISE-ready” means a facilitator can open a slide notebook in JupyterLab for presenter mode; students can read the same deck in the browser without installing RISE.
